# Mission

> **To give a software engineer the clearest, most complete view of their
> work, knowledge, and systems — from one self-hosted environment.**

---

## Mission Statement

DevOS exists to eliminate the friction of context-switching.

Every minute a developer spends searching for a bookmark, switching browser
tabs to check a deployment, opening a separate app to find a note, or
scrolling through Notion looking for a snippet — is a minute not spent
building.

DevOS's mission is to **reduce that friction to zero** by making every
relevant piece of context immediately visible, searchable, and actionable
from a single interface.

---

## What This Means in Practice

The mission succeeds when:

- A developer opens DevOS and knows the state of their system **in under 10 seconds**
- Every tool a developer reaches for daily is **one click or keystroke away**
- Knowledge is **never lost** — notes, snippets, bookmarks, and learnings
  are always findable
- Infrastructure, deployments, and server health are **visible without
  opening a separate dashboard**
- AI assistance is **context-aware** — it knows the projects, the stack,
  the standards

---

## Who This is For

**Primary:** Rizky — a software engineer who works across frontend, backend,
infrastructure, AI, security, and design.

**Secondary:** Any developer who wants a self-hosted personal operating system
they can fork, adapt, and own completely.

DevOS is not built for teams. It is not built for managers. It is built for
the individual engineer who wants full control over their environment.
